FAQ

Commercial painting questions

Can you work after hours or on weekends?
Yes. We regularly schedule commercial work around normal business hours, including evenings and weekends, to keep disruption to a minimum.
Can you work in phases to keep us open?
Yes. For larger spaces we can break the work into sections so parts of your building stay open and operational throughout the project.
Do you handle large exterior commercial buildings?
Yes, including block, metal, and siding exteriors. We'll walk the building and cover access and equipment needs in your written estimate.
Are you insured for commercial jobs?
Yes, Roca's Painting is fully insured. Ask us for details when we walk your property.
What's your warranty on commercial work?
Roca's Painting backs its work with a 1-year workmanship warranty. It covers issues with our application, like peeling or improper adhesion. It does not cover normal weather-related wear on exterior surfaces.
