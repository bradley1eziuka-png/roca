Preparation

A Beautiful Finish Starts Beneath the Paint

The quality of a painted surface begins long before the first coat is applied. We repair imperfections, address damaged surfaces, caulk where needed, and properly sand and prime each area to create the foundation for a smooth, uniform finish.

Protection

Your Home, Carefully Protected

Furniture, flooring, fixtures, and finished surfaces are carefully protected before work begins. We maintain an orderly work environment throughout the project and leave each space clean and ready for you to enjoy.

Color & Finish

The Right Finish for Every Space

Color is only part of the decision. We can help you select the appropriate sheen and coating for each room, considering the character of the space, how it is used, and the level of durability and finish you want to achieve.

FAQ

Interior painting questions

How long does a typical room take?
It depends on the room's size, how much prep and repair it needs, and the number of colors involved. We'll give you a specific timeline as part of your written estimate.
Do I need to move my furniture first?
No. We move furniture to the center of the room or cover it in place before we start, and cover your floors the same way.
Can you match an existing color?
Yes, in most cases. Bring us the can, the brand, or even just a sample from the wall and we'll work from there.
What's included in my quote?
Every written estimate spells out the prep work, number of coats, paint brand and sheen, and total price before we start.
What's your warranty?
Yes. Roca's Painting provides a one-year workmanship warranty covering application-related issues such as peeling or improper adhesion. Normal weathering and deterioration of the underlying surface are excluded.
