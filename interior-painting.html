Prep

The part most people never see

Nail holes get patched, cracks get caulked, and walls get sanded smooth before any paint goes on. It's slower than skipping straight to rolling color, and it's the difference between a paint job and a good paint job.

Protect

Your floors and furniture, covered before we start

  • Floors covered with drop cloths and protective film
  • Furniture moved to the center of the room or covered in place
  • Trim, outlets, and fixtures taped off with clean lines
  • Work area cleaned at the end of every day, not just the last one
Color & Finish

Guidance on sheen, not just color

The right sheen matters as much as the right color. We'll talk through what holds up in kitchens and bathrooms versus bedrooms and hallways, and point you toward options that fit how the room actually gets used.

Our Process

Every interior job follows the same six steps

Protect

Floors, furniture, and fixtures get covered before anything else happens.

Repair

Drywall holes and damaged trim get fixed and sanded smooth.

Scraping, sanding, patching, and priming, room by room.

FAQ

Interior painting questions

How long does a typical room take?
It depends on the room's size, how much prep and repair it needs, and the number of colors involved. We'll give you a specific timeline as part of your written estimate.
Do I need to move my furniture first?
No. We move furniture to the center of the room or cover it in place before we start, and cover your floors the same way.
Can you match an existing color?
Yes, in most cases. Bring us the can, the brand, or even just a sample from the wall and we'll work from there.
What's included in my quote?
Every written estimate spells out the prep work, number of coats, paint brand and sheen, and total price before we start.
What's your warranty?
Roca's Painting backs its work with a 1-year workmanship warranty. It covers issues with our application, like peeling or improper adhesion. It does not cover normal weather-related wear on exterior surfaces.
