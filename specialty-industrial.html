FAQ

Specialty & industrial questions

Do you work with general contractors and property managers?
Yes. We regularly coordinate with GCs, facility managers, and property management companies on scope, scheduling, and site access.
Can you work around our facility's operating hours?
Yes. Evening, weekend, and phased scheduling are all available so occupied facilities can keep operating during the work.
Can you provide product information before we approve the work?
Yes, we can provide product and material information for the coatings and systems we're proposing as part of your written quote.
Are you insured for commercial and industrial work?
Yes, Roca's Painting is fully insured. Ask us for details when we scope your project.
What's your warranty on specialty and industrial work?
Roca's Painting backs its work with a 1-year workmanship warranty. It covers issues with our application, like peeling or improper adhesion. It does not cover normal weather-related wear on exterior surfaces.
