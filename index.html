Most painting horror stories come down to three things: mess left behind, crews that don't show up when they say they will, and a final bill that doesn't match the estimate. Here's how we avoid all three.

Protect

Floors, furniture, and fixtures get covered before anything else happens.

How We Price

What actually determines your number

01

Prep and repair work

Drywall damage, stucco cracks, and heavy scraping add labor before paint ever goes on.

02

Access and height

Two-story exteriors, tall stairwells, and tight commercial spaces change equipment and time needed.

03

Occupied vs. vacant

Working around furniture, tenants, or business hours takes more coordination than an empty space.

04

Number of colors and coats

Multiple colors, accent walls, and higher-coverage paints all affect labor and material cost.

The only accurate number is a written one

FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

Do you offer free estimates?
Yes. Call 586-362-0208 or fill out the form below and we'll schedule a time to walk the project and send a written estimate. No pressure, no obligation.
What areas do you serve?
We're based in Lincoln Park and serve Metro Detroit, including Detroit, Lincoln Park, Southfield, and the surrounding communities. We've also completed projects throughout Southeast Michigan, including Monroe and Port Huron, for the right project.
Are you insured?
Yes, Roca's Painting is fully insured. Ask us for details when we walk your project.
What's included in my quote?
Every written estimate spells out the prep work, number of coats, paint brand and sheen, and total price before we start. Nothing gets added after the job begins without talking to you first.
How long does a typical project take?
It depends on the size of the job and how much prep and repair work is involved. We'll give you a specific timeline as part of your written estimate, before you commit to anything.
Will you protect my floors and furniture?
Yes. We cover floors, furniture, and fixtures before we open a single can of paint, and we clean up completely at the end of each work day.
Can you help me pick colors?
Yes. We can talk through finishes and sheens on-site and point you toward options that fit the space, the light, and how the room gets used.
What's your warranty?
Roca's Painting backs its work with a 1-year workmanship warranty. It covers issues with our application, like peeling or improper adhesion. It does not cover normal weather-related wear on exterior surfaces.
